# Search words to write for

The daily article writer picks one keyword from this list for each new article. A keyword is used once (as
the `keyword:` in an article's front matter). Long, specific phrases first: they are easier to rank for
than single words. Hindi and Hinglish searches matter as much as English.

Every article stays general information, with the doctor disclaimer, no medicine doses, no gender
prediction and no "genius baby" claims, whatever the keyword.

## Questions people ask AI assistants

Write a short "## Frequently asked questions" section (3–5 `###` questions with 2–3 sentence answers) at the
end of every guide. The site turns it into FAQ structured data for Google and AI answers. Phrase questions
as people type them: "Is it safe to …?", "When does …?", "What should I eat …?", "Kya pregnancy mein …?".
