# Content calendar: first 30 days (2 articles a day)

Status: **planned.** Starts after the wombtales.in launch (see LAUNCH_PLAN.md).

## Rules

- **Original writing only.** Never copy or lightly reword articles from other sites (copyright, and
  Google ranks copied pages lower). Competitor sites are used only to see _which topics_ people
  search for.
- Facts come from primary, trustworthy sources: WHO, NHS, MoHFW / National Health Mission India,
  FOGSI, ACOG, and the original scriptures for stories and shlokas. Cite them at the end of each
  article.
- Every health article ends with "Please check with your doctor", and is reviewed by a doctor before
  it is marked "Reviewed by Dr. \_\_\_".
- No IQ or "dream baby" claims, no gender prediction or son preference (PCPNDT Act), no fear.
- English and Hindi versions; 600–1,000 words; one clear keyword per article; link into the app
  ("Listen to this story in Wombtales").
- Slot A is usually a search-friendly guide; slot B is a story, ritual or calm piece that shows what
  makes Wombtales different.
