Privacy: your data stays on your phone

Wombtales was built because pregnancy apps kept asking for our data. So this one asks for nothing.

The short version

  • No account, no login. We never ask for your phone number or email.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Your dates, name, letters to baby, voice recordings, photos, moods, kicks and progress are saved only in your phone's browser storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no cookies.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because we never have it.

Backups

If you tap Backup, the app makes a file on your own phone. You decide where it goes (for example, your own Google Drive or WhatsApp to yourself). We never see it.

Reminders and the phone voice

Reminders are made by your phone. "Listen" uses your phone's own voice engine. Neither sends your data to us.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by a hosting service (Netlify). Like any website host, it may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security, for a short time. Wombtales itself does not collect or read them.

Deleting your data

Your data is yours. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, deletes it from your phone. Make a backup first if you want to keep your letters and photos.

Children and health information

Wombtales is for adults. Its health tips are general information, not medical advice. Please check with your doctor. Wombtales never predicts or helps choose a baby's sex (illegal in India under the PCPNDT Act).
