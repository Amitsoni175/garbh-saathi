# Content guide

_Full template and checklist come in Phase 3. The tone rules below apply from day one._

## Tone rules

- **Warm, simple, positive.** Short sentences. Talk the way a loving elder would.
- **No fear.** No scary images, no "if you don't do this…" language.
- **No gender preference.** Never predict or hope for a boy or a girl. No "putra prapti" content.
  Use "baby", "laado/laadla" together, or her nickname for the baby.
- **No medical promises** and no IQ claims. Frame everything as bonding, calm and joy.
  Health content always ends with "Please check with your doctor."
- **Respectful retellings.** Retell scriptures in your own words. Keep facts about real people accurate.
- **Hinglish** should be easy for any Hindi speaker to read, with a light Haryanvi touch in
  greetings ("Ram Ram ji", "Ghani badhiya!", "Shabaash!").
